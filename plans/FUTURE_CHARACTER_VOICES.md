# Future concept: voices bound to video characters

Deferred at the user's request on 2026-10-21. No installation or generation integration is approved by this note.

Desired outcome: assign Saved voice A to on-screen character A and another saved voice to character B, preserving identity and synchronized speaking performances. A backing narration track alone does not satisfy this goal.

Our installed FreeVideo code has experimental audio references with an explicit warning that they do not lock the soundtrack or lip sync. A future proof of concept should evaluate separate per-character dialogue tracks with audio-driven multi-person generation, for example MultiTalk/InfiniteTalk, or another maintained compatible model. Validate quality, character-to-speaker binding, local licensing, storage, and actual runtime/memory on the 8 GiB RTX 3060 Ti before committing. Keep generation and references local unless separately authorized.
